# Safe Card Pilot — PRC Content Approval Request

**To:** Philippine Red Cross (program owner / DPO where applicable)
**From:** Safe Card MVP pilot team (Eric and Indy Paragas, with VibeCodersPH)
**Date:** 28 August 2026
**Subject:** Content and data approval for the Safe Card MVP pilot (5-day sprint)

## Purpose

We are building a mobile-first referral and application-intake pilot for the
Safe Card program. The pilot tests whether student advocates can introduce
Safe Card to household helpers, drivers, caregivers, and similar support
workers through a QR/link referral, whether plain-language benefit
explanations help recipients decide, and whether a simple intake form reduces
friction. A submission is treated as referral/application intake only, never
automatic enrollment or coverage, unless PRC formally confirms otherwise.

All public-facing content below is currently sample placeholder text. The
pilot will not collect real applicant data until this list is approved.

## Items requiring approval

1. **Program pricing statement.** Confirm the official current wording and
   price for Safe Card (sample currently in the build: ₱1,200 per year or
   ₱100 per month). Provide the exact sentence to display.

2. **Benefit statements.** Confirm the official wording for the four benefits
   referenced in the pilot: (a) emergency ambulance service, (b) one unit of
   whole blood, (c) accident-related hospitalization coverage, (d)
   dengue-related assistance. Please provide any eligibility conditions that
   must appear alongside each benefit.

3. **Application fields.** Confirm the intake form fields. The pilot currently
   requests: full name, mobile number, city or barangay, and household role
   (kasambahay / driver / caregiver / other). Add, remove, or rename as
   required by the official application.

4. **Privacy notice and consent language.** Approve the plain-language notice
   shown before submission, the consent checkbox wording, and the retention
   period for pilot records. We also ask PRC to confirm the Personal
   Information Controller (PIC) and Personal Information Processor (PIP)
   roles for this pilot under RA 10173.

5. **Logo and program name usage.** Confirm use of the Philippine Red Cross
   logo and the Safe Card program name on pilot pages and advocate posters,
   for the pilot period only.

6. **Intake boundary statement.** Confirm the wording that a submission is
   application intake and not automatic enrollment or coverage, and the
   expected next step a recipient will experience after submitting.

## Timeline

The five-day sprint starts once the proposal is accepted. Items 1 to 6 are
needed before Day 1 so the build can use approved content instead of
placeholders. If approval is not ready by the validation day, the pilot will
run with synthetic test data only, as agreed in the proposal.

## Technical readiness (no action needed from PRC)

The pilot runs on a private preview environment with access controls. Form
data is stored with least-privilege rules and individual credentials, and no
applicant data is sent to external AI tools. An escalation path for any
suspected incident or breach is in place.
